About

Farming Essentials is a Minecraft mod project centered on the simple pleasure of semi-automating Farming.

Thoughtful additions, one harvest at a time.

The goal is to make farming feel less boring and repettetive while keeping your world your own. This site is made from the Owner of Farming Essentials.

Have a question about installation or compatibility? Visit the FAQ before setting up the mod.
