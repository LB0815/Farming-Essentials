Growing a good community

Rules

A few simple guidelines help keep project conversations useful and welcoming.

01

Be respectful

Keep discussions constructive, and treat other players and contributors with courtesy.

02

Keep feedback useful

When reporting an issue, include the relevant Minecraft, loader, and mod versions when known.

03

Share responsibly

Use the project’s release files and respect the terms that apply to the mod and its assets.
